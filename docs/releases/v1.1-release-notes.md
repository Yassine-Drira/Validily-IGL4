## Summary of Major Features & Improvements

### 1. Focus Forest Visual Overhaul (Bosquet Focus)
- **Illustrated Isometric Diorama Canvas:** Replaced flat green canvas with a 2.5D floating isometric island ($6 \times 6$ diamond grid, 36 plots).
- **Subterranean Cliff Foundations:** Multi-layered 3D earth strata with warm geological bands, organic dangling roots, and overhanging grass trim.
- **6 Distinct Handcrafted Species:**
  - *Atlas Cedar* (evergreen needle tiers & mountain resilience)
  - *Heritage Oak* (deep canopy & academic permanence)
  - *Stone Pine* (umbrella crown & Mediterranean warmth)
  - *Sage Willow* (cascading silver-green foliage)
  - *Flamboyant Jacaranda* (vibrant purple-violet blossoms)
  - *Zen Sakura* (soft pink cherry blossoms with floating petal ground scatter)
- **Leafless Tree State:** Unique deadwood silhouette for abandoned sessions with realistic dry branches and fallen leaves.
- **Biome Seasonal System:** Summer Meadow, Autumn Orchard, Winter Frost, and Spring Sakura.
- **Micro-Decorations:** Wild chamomile, river stones, and ground glints so that even a 0-tree or 1-tree island feels complete and alive.
- **Isometric Depth Sorting:** Mathematically accurate depth ordering preventing clipping or sprite overlap.

### 2. HTTP 431 Elimination & Next.js 16 SSR Auth Proxy
- **Unified Auth Proxy (`src/proxy.ts`):** Strips redundant header bloat on SSR requests while preserving session security.
- **Dynamic Cookie Compaction (`src/lib/auth/cookie-utils.ts`):** Detects and prunes orphaned chunk cookies on session renewal; bounds header size under 8KB limit.
- **Presigned Storage Cache (`src/lib/supabase/storage-cache.ts`):** In-memory cache for Supabase Storage signed URLs, eliminating redundant queries.

### 3. Security Hardening & Zero-Knowledge Isolation (Phase 0)
- **Database RLS Policies:** Strict separation between private personal notes, draft quizzes, and community resources.
- **Server Actions Access Gate:** Enforced authentication checks on mutations; guests cannot mutate database state.
- **Adversarial Test Suite:** 100% verified against cross-tenant ID snooping and unauthenticated privilege escalation.

### 4. Interactive Focus Hub & Ambient Audio
- **Autonomous Web Audio Synthesizer:** Rain, ocean waves, white noise, and lo-fi cafe generator with zero external audio assets.
- **Pomodoro Progression:** Live seedling growth stages (0, 1, 2, 3) mapped to active focus timer.
